HVAC Maintenance Services

Regular maintenance is the key to keeping your HVAC system running efficiently and reliably. At Nash Heating & Air Conditioning, we offer comprehensive maintenance programs designed to extend the life of your equipment, improve energy efficiency, and prevent costly breakdowns.

Our Maintenance Services Cover

Heat Pumps: Comprehensive inspections and tune-ups for optimal performance

Ductless Mini-Splits: Filter cleaning, refrigerant checks, and system optimization

Central AC Systems: Complete system checks, coil cleaning, and performance testing

Furnaces: Safety inspections, burner adjustments, and efficiency improvements

What's Included in Our Maintenance Program

Thorough System Inspection

Complete evaluation of all HVAC components to identify potential issues before they become major problems.

Filter Replacement & Cleaning

Clean or replace air filters to maintain optimal air quality and system efficiency.

Calibration & Adjustments

Fine-tune thermostats, check refrigerant levels, and adjust system settings for peak performance.

Safety Checks

Comprehensive safety inspections including electrical connections, gas lines, and carbon monoxide testing.

Priority Service

Maintenance program members receive priority scheduling and special discounts on repairs.

Benefits of Regular Maintenance

Lower Energy Bills: Well-maintained systems run more efficiently, saving you money

Fewer Breakdowns: Catch problems early and avoid unexpected system failures

Extended Lifespan: Regular care helps your HVAC system last longer

Improved Comfort: Consistent performance means better temperature control

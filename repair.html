Signs You Need HVAC Repair

  • System won't turn on or cycles on and off frequently
  • Weak airflow or no air coming from vents
  • Unusual noises like grinding, squealing, or banging
  • Strange odors when system is running
  • Thermostat not responding or temperature inconsistencies
  • Visible leaks or moisture around the unit
  • Sudden increase in energy bills without explanation
  • System blowing warm air when it should be cooling (or vice versa)
